## Overall assessment

- **FERC order**: trade press got the citation, date, and substance of the three transmission services right. The one real gap is docket-number completeness (two additional consolidated dockets, AD24-11-000 and EL25-20-000, are usually omitted) and the implicit claim of finality — **the compliance process is still actively iterating as of Aug 2026** (April 16 and June 18, 2026 follow-on orders each partially rejected PJM's filings and demanded more revisions), which any write-up citing this order should flag rather than treating Dec 18, 2025 as the end of the story.
- **Virginia SCC**: both case numbers (PUR-2025-00058 biennial review; PUR-2026-00056 Rider T-1) and the GS-5 rate-class mechanics are confirmed against the SCC's own materials. The Rider T-1 dollar figures and 90-day direct-connect directive rest on strong secondary corroboration rather than a directly-fetched order PDF — worth a follow-up primary-source pull if precision on those exact numbers matters for the paper.
- **PJM RTEP**: board approval date and the ~$11.8B baseline figure (precisely $11,840.24M, with $12,241.25M being the full net RTEP action that day) are confirmed directly from PJM's own whitepaper. The Dominion HVDC-specific figures are well-corroborated across five outlets but not independently pulled from PJM's own cost tables in this pass; note the 185-vs-186-mile discrepancy across sources.
